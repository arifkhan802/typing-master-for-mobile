Online Urdu typing test and practice

Practice Urdu typing online with Urdu paragraphs and letter lessons. This is a free Urdu typing test with live speed and accuracy, useful for Urdu typing practice and job test preparation. Try the Urdu typing test for 5 minutes or the LDC and UDC practice page.

You type with your own Urdu keyboard. Translation and conversion tools such as English to Urdu or Hindi to Urdu typing are not included; this page is for typing practice.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an Urdu keyboard?

Yes. Add an Urdu keyboard on your phone, for example in Gboard, or use a physical Urdu keyboard.

Is there a phonetic keyboard?

Not yet. This page tests typing with your own Urdu keyboard.

Can I take a 5 minute Urdu test?

Yes, choose 5 minutes or open the 5 minute page.

Is this InPage typing?

No. It is an online typing test for Urdu text. InPage is separate software.

Does it work offline?

Yes, download the Urdu offline file on the Download page.

Is it free?

Yes, it is free.
