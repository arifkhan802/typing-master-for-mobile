Why typing speed matters

Typing is one of the most useful computer skills. Students use it for homework and online exams, office workers use it for emails and reports, and job seekers face typing tests for clerk, assistant and data entry posts. A person who types 40 words per minute finishes the same work in half the time of someone who types 20.

Accuracy matters as much as speed, because every mistake takes time to fix. That is why our tests show both your words per minute (WPM) and your accuracy, and why our lessons teach clean, steady typing first. Speed follows naturally when your fingers know where every key is.

Some tips for typing faster

1

Learn the correct finger position

Place your fingers on the home row and use the finger chart.
2

Learn to type without looking

Keep your eyes on the screen, not on the keys.
3

Avoid correcting mistakes immediately

Keep your rhythm. Finish the word, then fix the habit behind the mistake.
4

Type faster for short periods

Try short bursts of speed, then return to calm, accurate typing.
5

Sit in a proper posture

Good posture protects your shoulders and back during long sessions.
